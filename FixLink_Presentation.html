Context & Motivation
🏠
The Everyday Reality of Home Repairs

Every household routinely requires urgent or scheduled repair services—ranging from plumbing leaks and electrical faults to appliance repairs and carpentry.

  • ✓ Unpredictable home breakdowns require rapid, reliable intervention.
  • ✓ Traditional search depends on informal word-of-mouth or paper flyers.
  • ✓ Lack of verified technician credentials creates safety and quality concerns.
💡
Bete: The Digital Paradigm

Bete bridges the physical service gap by introducing a mobile-first digital marketplace that unites homeowners and certified local technicians into a unified ecosystem.

  • ✓ Seamless Matching: Instant request broadcast to specialized nearby workers.
  • ✓ Competitive Bidding: Transparent quotation model eliminating price gouging.
  • ✓ Accountability: Verified user profiles, live in-app chat, and transparent ratings.

Need Analysis

Problem Statement

Four major friction points in conventional household repair arrangements
Core Challenges
🔍
1. Difficulty Finding Trusted Technicians

Homeowners struggle to find reliable, vetted handymen promptly during emergencies. Informal networks offer zero background checks or skill verification.

📋
2. Information Asymmetry

Customers possess virtually no visibility into a technician's past performance, genuine certifications, portfolio, or verified availability.

💬
3. Friction in Price & Communication

Unstandardized pricing leads to arbitrary overcharging. Phone negotiations lack audit trails, documented scope, and mutual confirmation.

⭐
4. Absence of Tracking & Reviews

Once hired, work progress cannot be monitored systematically. If work is substandard, customers have no recourse or platform feedback loop.

System Capabilities

Main Features

Comprehensive functional modules engineered within the mobile platform
10 Core Modules
User Facing & Transactional
  • 🔐 Auth & Role Management: Secure registration, JWT sessions, role separation (Customer, Provider, Admin).
  • 📂 Service Taxonomy: Categorized offerings (Plumbing, Electrical, Carpentry, Appliance repair) with sub-services.
  • 📋 Request Posting & Search: Dynamic job creation with description, photos, schedules, and provider filtering.
  • 💰 Quotation & Bidding Engine: Providers propose rates; customers evaluate and hire in one click.
  • 📦 Order & Task Lifecycle: Real-time task tracking across Pending, In-Progress, and Completed states.
Communication, Trust & Operations
  • 💬 Real-Time Messaging: WebSockets (Socket.io) chat with immediate duplex delivery and chat history.
  • 🔔 Push & In-App Alerts: Immediate notifications for bid receipts, acceptance, and messaging events.
  • ⭐ Verified Rating System: Star ratings and qualitative feedback locked strictly to completed orders.
  • 👤 Profile & Portfolio: Bio, skills list, Cloudinary profile photo, and operational stats.
  • 📊 Administrative Oversight: User management, service catalogs, dispute control, and platform KPIs.
